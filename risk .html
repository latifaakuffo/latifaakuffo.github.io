AI Tool Control Testing — POCA 2002

Completed specialist finance domain AI annotation work (DataAnnotation, 2026) evaluating AI-generated financial crime compliance outputs against POCA 2002 statutory provisions. Identified a four-stage failure pattern:

Error 1 — False Precision: AI stated moratorium period as 7 calendar days. Correct answer: 7 working days under s.335 POCA 2002.
Error 2 — Wrong Statutory Provision: AI cited s.333 for tipping off. Correct provision: s.333A as inserted by SOCPA 2005.
Error 3 — False Uncertainty: AI retreated to "I am not sure" when challenged, despite errors being verifiable statutory facts.
Error 4 — Misapplied Knowledge Cutoff: AI used January 2025 cutoff as excuse for pre-2005 statutory provisions.
Outcome: Overall model performance rated Poor. Structured evaluation forms submitted with written rationale for each error stage. This expertise applies directly to evaluating AI-driven compliance and fraud detection tools.
